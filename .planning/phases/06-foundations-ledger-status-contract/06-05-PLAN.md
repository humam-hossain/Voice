<objective>
Instrument honest timing and make transcription incremental: one timed seam around the full faster-whisper generator consumption, per-chunk decoder evidence, GPU→CPU fallback instrumentation, a model that loads while the user is still speaking, and chunk-by-chunk decoding during recording — then write all of it through the schema-v1 record builder.

Purpose: Success criterion 3 depends on rows whose `decode_ms` is not a lie and on the model being hot when the user stops. Today the model loads after the WAV is finalized (the post-stop cliff), `transcribe()` stops the clock before consuming the generator, and the ledger row carries no evidence.
Output: `transcribe_timed()` + `TranscriptionEvidence` (including per-chunk `audio_s`), `Recorder.on_chunk_ready(idx, path, audio_s)`, a transcription consumer thread that starts right after `recorder.start()` and publishes `recording/load|decode` until `capture_closed`, stage timings and per-chunk evidence in the ledger row, and two new test modules plus mechanical mock migration in three existing modules.
</objective>
